PGBR Presidential History

The Presidents of the PGBR empire have ruled over the 3 empires, 2 republics and the xClan Union. They serve in the second highest political role in the empire below the King. Since May 2020 there have been 10 presidencies occupied by 9 people, including some unrecognized ones like Odie's claim to the presidency in 2022 and Colton's claim to the presidency in 2022 during New PGBR.

History

The presidency of PGBR was established in 2020 following a revolution that led to the founding of the republic. This revolution marked the end of an era characterized by a tribal government, ushering in a new phase of self-governance and democratic processes. The creation of the presidential office was a cool FUCKIN moment in orur country's history, symbolizing the aspirations for a stable and democratic government.
